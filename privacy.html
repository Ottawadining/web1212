Privacy

We keep very little,and sell nothing.

Updated November 2026

Looking around

You can browse Plated without an account. We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies, and we don’t build profiles of visitors.

Plates you save

When you save a plate to your Collection without an account, the list is kept only in your own browser. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Accounts

An account is optional. If you sign in with Google or by email, we keep your email address and the list of plates you’ve saved, so your collection appears on every device where you sign in. From Google we receive only your name, email address and profile picture, and we use them for nothing but signing you in.

Submissions

If you suggest a dish, we keep what you send (the restaurant, the dish, your contact email and the photograph) to review it. Your email is never published. To prevent spam, we keep a scrambled, one-way code made from your internet address, never the address itself.

Who else is involved

Plated is hosted on GitHub Pages, its data is stored with Supabase, and sign-in emails are sent by an email delivery service. They handle this information only to run the site for us.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time from your Collection. To delete your account and saved plates, or a submission, write to hello@plated.gallery and we’ll remove them.
